Logging as

campus has no accounts or passwords. This name is just a label on your transcript, like writing your name on a notebook. Use the same name on another device to share a transcript, or a different name to keep them apart. There is nothing sensitive stored here, only what you watched, so anyone who knows the name can read that transcript. Treat it as a label, not a secret.
